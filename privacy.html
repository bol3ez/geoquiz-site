GeoQuiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

GeoQuiz ("the app") is a geography quiz game for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data stored on your device

GeoQuiz works offline. Your profile (chosen name and avatar), level, coins, gems, energy, streaks, statistics, achievements and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded by the app. Deleting the app or using "Reset Progress" in Settings erases them.

Data we do not collect

The app does not ask for an account, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos. It does not read your files or track you across other apps.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are requested in non-personalised mode. Google may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and manage your advertising ID in the Android settings ("Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation").

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch them in exchange for in-game energy or coins.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

In-app purchases

If the app offers purchases (gem packs or removing ads), payment is processed by Google Play. The app only receives a confirmation of the purchase and never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder in Settings, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification server is involved and the reminder can be turned off at any time.

Sharing features

"Challenge a friend" and "Invite friends" create a text message with a link that you choose to share through apps on your device. GeoQuiz does not see who you share it with.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown to users who may be children are requested as non-personalised ads.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app gains new features. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy or about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/bol3ez/geoquiz-site/issues. Please do not post personal information there; describe your question and we will reply.
